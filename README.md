## 🚀 Featured Project

### Focux Personal

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://focuxpersonal.com">
    <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Visit%20Focux%20Personal-111111?style=for-the-badge&logo=googlechrome&logoColor=white" alt="Visit Focux Personal" />

**Focux Personal** is a SaaS platform designed for personal trainers, combining **mobile technology, business management, data and artificial intelligence** into a single ecosystem.

I created and developed the product from the ground up, working across the application's architecture, backend, mobile experience, database and intelligent features.

### Built with

`Java` · `Spring Boot` · `Flutter` · `Dart` · `PostgreSQL` · `Redis` · `Firebase` · `Docker` · `AI`

### Product focus

* 👤 Client & personal trainer management
* 📊 Performance & data-driven features
* 🏋️ Training management
* 💰 Business & financial features
* 🤖 AI-powered experiences
* 📱 Cross-platform mobile experience
* 🔐 Authentication & secure APIs
* ☁️ Cloud-based architecture
